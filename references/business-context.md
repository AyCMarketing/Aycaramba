**Source:** https://www.aycaramba.at/
**Last updated:** 2026-07-23

## Business Fundamentals

- **Name:** AyCaramba (also styled "Ay Caramba!")
- **Business Type:** Marketing agency
- **Location:** Klosterneuburg, just outside Vienna, Austria ("direkt vor den Toren Wiens")
- **Service Model:** Hands-on strategy combined with efficient, AI-accelerated execution

## Services Offered

Three pillars, framed as one integrated system ("Drei Bereiche, ein System"):

**1. Umsatz-Wahnsinn (Customer Acquisition)**
- Konzeption — end-to-end strategy for digital customer acquisition
- Kampagnen — paid campaigns, landing pages, automated lead-magnet delivery
- Content Erstellung — social media content, AI-assisted production at scale

**2. Website-Perfektion (Websites)**
- Webdesign — distinctive, non-generic design
- Webentwicklung: WordPress — configurable, client-owned builds
- Webentwicklung: individuelle Websites — fully custom builds
- Shopentwicklung — e-commerce/shop builds
- SEO & SEA — visibility and traffic generation
- Content & Wartung — ongoing content updates, maintenance, security

**3. KI-Irrsinn (AI)**
- AI Education — training for individuals and marketing teams
- AI Prozessoptimierung — automating recurring business processes
- Digitale Produktentwicklung (SaaS) — building a client's own SaaS product from idea to first revenue

**In-house SaaS products (built and run by AyCaramba itself):**
- **Peppi** — social media content tool for German-speaking businesses (idea generation, multi-platform post creation, content calendar)
- **Vega** — AI tool that detects real-time purchase intent on websites and uses AI agents to actively convert undecided visitors, not just advise; positioned as a standing partner product included with engagements

## Target Market

Small and medium-sized businesses across a range of industries (legal, hospitality, healthcare/assistive tech, personal training/fitness, real estate, corporate B2B), looking for:
- Predictable, plannable customer acquisition
- A website that actually converts, not just looks good
- Time savings through AI-driven automation ("Marketing auf Autopilot")

Addressed informally (German "du") — the tone targets founders/owners who want fast, direct results over long theoretical engagements.
